How to View the AR Apartment

No marker, no printout, no app — just your phone camera

✓ No marker needed — works on any flat surface!

Quick Start

  1. Open the AR link — scan the QR code on the brochure, or tap the link shared on WhatsApp.
  2. Allow camera access — your browser will ask once. Tap Allow.
  3. Tap "Start AR" — the button appears on screen after the page loads.
  4. Aim at a flat surface — a gold ring appears when a surface is detected. Works on tables, floors, counters, even paper.
  5. Tap to place — the 3D apartment model appears on the surface. Walk around it to explore every room!
  6. Move it — tap the ↺ Move button to pick it up and place it somewhere else.

Tips for Best Results

  • Use in a well-lit room — avoid very dark environments.
  • Move the phone slowly over the surface before tapping.
  • Plain surfaces (white table, grey floor) detect faster than patterned ones.
  • Hold the phone at a slight downward angle (not straight down).
  • On Android, make sure ARCore is installed (it's free from Play Store).
  • On iPhone, use Safari on iOS 16.4 or newer.
