How to View Hari Enclave in AR

  1. Tap Start AR and allow camera access.
  2. Wait for the 3D model to finish loading (first time only).
  3. Point your phone at a flat surface — table or floor.
  4. A gold ring appears when a surface is detected.
  5. Tap to place the building on that spot.
  6. Walk around it to explore all sides and angles.
  7. Tap ↺ Move to pick it up and reposition.
