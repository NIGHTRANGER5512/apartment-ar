How to View Hari Enclave in AR

  1. Tap Start AR and allow camera access.
  2. Wait briefly for the 3D model to load (first time).
  3. Point at a flat surface — table or floor.
  4. A gold ring appears when a surface is detected.
  5. Tap to place the building on that spot.
  6. Walk around to explore all sides and angles.
  7. Tap ↺ Move to pick up and reposition.
