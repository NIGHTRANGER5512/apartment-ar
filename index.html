Hari Enclave AR — Point camera at a flat surface

How to View in AR

  1. Tap Start AR below.
  2. Allow camera access.
  3. Slowly move your phone over a flat surface (table, floor).
  4. A white ring appears — tap anywhere to place the building.
  5. Tap again to reposition it.

💡 Works on Android Chrome. iPhone needs the GitHub Pages URL (HTTPS).

Tap Start AR → move phone over a flat surface → tap to place

⚠️ AR Not Supported

Your browser doesn't support WebXR AR.

Android: Use Chrome 81+ with ARCore installed.

iPhone: Open this page in Safari on iOS 16+ via HTTPS.

Deploy to GitHub Pages for HTTPS access.
