How to Use the AR Viewer

📱 iPhone (iOS 12+)

  1. Tap the gold View in AR button.
  2. Safari opens AR Quick Look — allow camera access.
  3. Point at a flat surface (floor or table).
  4. Tap to place Hari Enclave on the surface.
  5. Pinch to resize · drag to move · tap ✕ to exit.

🧟 Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the gold View in AR button.
  2. Allow camera access when prompted.
  3. Point at a flat surface until a ring appears.
  4. Tap to place the building on that spot.
  5. Walk around to explore every angle.

📌 3D Viewer (any device)

  1. Drag to rotate the model.
  2. Pinch or scroll to zoom in and out.
  3. The model auto-rotates — drag to take control.
