Step 3 — Share with Customers

✓ No marker or printout needed — just the QR code!
  • Print on brochure: Place the QR code anywhere on the brochure. Minimum size 3 × 3 cm. Customer scans → camera opens → taps any flat surface → apartment appears.
  • Share on WhatsApp: Tap Download QR, then send the PNG image in your WhatsApp chat or status. Customers tap the QR image to open the link.
  • Share URL directly: Tap Copy Link and paste it into any WhatsApp or SMS message. Customers tap the link to open it instantly.
  • Customer flow: Scan QR → browser opens → allow camera → point at a flat surface → tap → 3D apartment appears!
